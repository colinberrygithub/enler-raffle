The small print

How the draw works
  • Each number is a unique, randomly generated 6-digit number.
  • Numbers are only assigned after your payment has been received and matched to your details.
  • Entries close at 11:59pm on Sunday 13 December 2026. Payments or forms received after that are not entered and will be refunded.
  • The winning number is drawn at random on Monday 14 December 2026, and the winner is contacted by phone and email.
  • The prize is two tickets to Anto Cacace vs Nick Ball in Belfast. There is no cash alternative.
  • There is no limit on how many numbers you can buy.
Your details
